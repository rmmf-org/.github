# Rouben Malian Memorial Foundation

**Honoring a legacy. Investing in what people can become.**

Potential is universal. Opportunity is not. The Rouben Malian Memorial Foundation exists to close the distance between
the two.

Founded in New York in 2018 in memory of Rouben Malian, the Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that builds and funds a
few initiatives in full, rather than many thinly, and stays with them for the long term. Its work follows Rouben's
values: integrity, tenacity, and a willingness to take big bets on people and ideas.

## Flagship initiatives

Two initiatives, wholly funded by the Foundation.

### [Admit Path](https://www.admitpath.org)

Evidence-based educational guidance for learners from the age of eleven to eighty and beyond: high school, college and
graduate admissions, career changes and learning in later life. Its advice combines what institutions say they value
with what actually drives admission, reviewed by independent human readers and overseen by an Ethics Board.

- Free Lite readiness assessment for everyone, with no account required
- Sliding scale from $0, free below the federal poverty line
- Every five full-price reports fund one for someone who could not otherwise afford it
- Never writes essays, never guarantees admission, never sells personal data

[admitpath.org](https://www.admitpath.org) · [About Admit Path](https://www.roubenmalianmemorial.org/admitpath)

### [R.G. Menzies Scholarship to Harvard Alumni Group](https://www.rgmenziesharvard.org)

Since 1968, more than 100 Australians have studied at Harvard as R.G. Menzies Scholars, on one defining expectation:
that they bring what they learn home. The Foundation fully funds the Alumni Group that keeps them connected: its
digital home, the private Scholar Network, and the mentoring that links each generation of scholars to the next.

The Scholarship itself is funded by the Harvard Club of Australia and administered by the Australian National
University, which selects scholars through a joint panel.

[rgmenziesharvard.org](https://www.rgmenziesharvard.org) · [About the Alumni Group](https://www.roubenmalianmemorial.org/menzies)

## How we work

- **Issues** use the organization's issue forms: Bug, Feature, Task, Epic and Initiative. Each sets an issue type.
- **Hierarchy:** Initiative (a portfolio-level outcome over a quarter or more) > Epic (a shippable body of work) > Feature, Task or Bug, linked as sub-issues. Blocking work is recorded as issue dependencies.
- **Project boards:** every product has a GitHub Project ("Rouben Malian Memorial Foundation · <Product>") with Status (Backlog, Ready, In progress, In review, Done), Priority (P0–P3), Size (XS–XL) and two-week iterations.
- **Pull requests** follow the pull request template and close their issue with "Closes #n".
- **Security:** please report vulnerabilities privately to [support@roubenmalianmemorial.org](mailto:support@roubenmalianmemorial.org), not in a public issue.
